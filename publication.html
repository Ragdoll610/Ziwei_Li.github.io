[1]  Ziwei Li, Masahiro Hirano, Qitong Guo, Ruoyu Jia, Yuji Yamakawa. SG-NLOSTrack: Semantic-Guided Non-Line-of-Sight Target Tracking. Submitted to IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Conference (ITSC), 2025 (under review).

Brief Introduction：This paper proposes SG-NLOSTrack, a semantic-guided dual-branch framework for 4D radar-based tracking of non-line-of-sight (NLOS) targets. By integrating image semantics and point cloud processing, the method estimates NLOS target positions through reflection geometry and tracks them over time using a Kalman filter. Evaluated on the UT-NLOS Dataset, SG-NLOSTrack achieves a mean absolute error of 0.419 m and a total tracking success rate of 80%, demonstrating its effectiveness in urban NLOS scenarios.

[2]  Ziwei Li, Liangqi Gui*, Huimin Xiong, Cheng Ke, Lang Liang. Millimeter-Wave Radiation Model and Experiment of the Kelvin Wake. The 29th Annual Electronic Warfare Three New Academic Conference, Xi’an, China, September 2021.

Brief Introduction：A millimeter-wave radiation model of ship wake is proposed based on point source perturbation theory and the Kirchhoff Approximation. The Kelvin wake of ships with varying speeds and sizes is modeled, and the sea surface is discretized into small plane elements. The brightness temperature and radiation characteristics are analyzed under different observation conditions. Experimental validation shows that the relative error between simulation and measurement is within 10%.
