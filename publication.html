[1] Ziwei Li, Liangqi Gui*, Huimin Xiong, Cheng Ke, Lang Liang. Millimeter-Wave Radiation Model and Experiment of the Kelvin Wake. The 29th Annual Electronic Warfare Three New Academic Conference, Xi’an, China, September 2021.

A millimeter-wave radiation model of ship wake is established based on Kelvin wake theory and Kirchhoff approximation, with experimental validation showing ~10% error.
