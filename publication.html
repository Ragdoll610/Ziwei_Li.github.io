[1]  Ziwei Li, Liangqi Gui*, Huimin Xiong, Cheng Ke, Lang Liang. Millimeter-Wave Radiation Model and Experiment of the Kelvin Wake. The 29th Annual Electronic Warfare Three New Academic Conference, Xi’an, China, September 2021.

A millimeter-wave radiation model of ship wake is proposed based on point source perturbation theory and the Kirchhoff Approximation. The Kelvin wake of ships with varying speeds and sizes is modeled, and the sea surface is discretized into small plane elements. The brightness temperature and radiation characteristics are analyzed under different observation conditions. Experimental validation shows that the relative error between simulation and measurement is within 10%.
